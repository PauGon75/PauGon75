About me

I'm Pau González Cayuela, a technical game designer based in Barcelona who works where design meets code. I design gameplay systems, combat and progression, then prototype, implement and tune them myself in Unreal Engine 4/5, Unity, Godot and custom engines.

My toolkit covers game balance (modeled in Machination and Excel), game feel polish, level design and QA, backed by C#, C++ and GDScript plus Assembly. I write clear design documentation and keep teams aligned from prototype to release.

I have worked as Technical Designer, Lead Game Designer and Level Designer, co-founded 75 Studio, and taught review sessions in Game Mathematics and Game Programming at UPC. My work has earned university honor titles and game jam awards.

Outside of work, I'm passionate about technology and development, and I love cinema and cooking. I'm also a big fan of old-school FPS shooters.

The Fable of the Eclipse

Technical Designer · Mini-Knight Studio (Jan - Jul 2026)

In this project I've specially learned and focused on the bridge between design and programming. How conceptual ideas can be applied onto a game framework. Doing it fast and sustainable to iterate things as soon as posible. Also, breaking down things a part to see what is actually failing and how to improve it's feeling.

"Simplicity is a great virtue but it requires hard work to achieve it and education to appreciate it. And to make things worse: complexity sells better."

Combat design and implementation

I designed and implemented a combat framework in the game. Also, making the player have reactions like knockback, making the enemies shine when hit, slowdown the combo, and so many more polishments!

Player Movement

I was in charge of making the character able to move in an isometric view, also implementing the Dash ability. And as a cherry on top, I improved the feeling via haptic design and buffers.

Map implementation

I implemented the lava map inside the engine, and creating things such as cutscenes, particles... To improve the overall experience.

Perks creation

I was in charge of designing, iterating and creating both Grapple and Torch perks. The grapple has a complex rope system that works with a sinusoidal wave of meshes that stretch themselves based on the distance that the player is from a pillar.

Polish

I did different kind of polishes in the overall game. That include: haptic polishes, time slows, some cutscene, lively grass...

Enemy Design

I designed the slime and the golem behaviours and elemental implications. All of them using flowcharts to grant a proper communication.

QA Playtest

As part of the ongoing development for Fable Of The Eclipse, a structured playtesting phase has been completed to refine the core gameplay experience.
