01 // INTRODUCTION

About Me & Skills

Technical Designer specializing in gameplay systems, combat feel, and in-engine implementation.

Based in Barcelona, I bridge the gap between creative design vision and engineering pipelines. I design, prototype, and tune core gameplay mechanics focusing on state machines, dynamic parameters, and mathematical balance (Machinations and Excel) in Unreal Engine (C++/Blueprints), Unity (C#) and Godot (GDscript).

Having worked across gameplay programming, technical design, and QA as well as teaching Game Mathematics and Programming at UPC. I bring a strong architectural foundation to design teams, ensuring systems are both deeply satisfying to play and performant under the hood.

Outside of work, I'm passionate about technology and development, and I love cinema and cooking. I'm also a big fan of old-school FPS shooters.
